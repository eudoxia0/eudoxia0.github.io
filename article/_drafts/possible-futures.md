# Questions

- **Do we reach ASI?** Do we reach the point where AI is massively better than
  humans at almost all cognitive tasks, or does the intelligence explosion
  fizzle out, and produce minds that have very jagged capabilities, and are
  worse than humans at some things?
- **Do we solve alignment?** Can we instill our own values into the AI, or does
  it have its own evolved, ahuman value system?
- **What kind of alignment?** Intent alignment means an AI that reliably follows
  the intent of its operator (that is: only takes actions the operator would
  approve of), value alignment means aligning to an impersonal system of values.
- **Takeoff severity?** If the AI is unaligned, how fatal is that for humans? Do
  we get an out-of-control paperclip maximizer, or a more benign AI that is
  unaligned but which cares enough to stay out of our way?
- **How many ASIs?** A [singleton][sing], a pantheon, or an ecology?
- **Full automation?** In the no-ASI case, do we still automate almost all jobs
  with non-superintelligent AI?

# Misuse Doom {#misuse-doom}

In this world, an AI ends the world by following the orders of its operator. The
AI might be strongly intent-aligned, so that it does whatever the operator asks;
or it might be an open-source model with refusals ablated away; or it might be a
commercial AI where safeguards are circumvented by clever task decomposition.

This could probably happen today: a pandemic could cause human extinction, and
AI is capable enough to assist in biology research. The obvious example of
biorisk is a terrorist group using AI to make a bioweapon. But you could get to
this outcome without anyone even trying to make a bioweapon. A
government-funded, completely official and legitimate laboratory doing
gain-of-function research on some pathogen could accidentally start a
pandemic. In this scenario, the AI's role in the causal chain would be:

- Compressing timelines by speeding up literature review, hypothesis selection,
  experiment design, data analysis, equipment scheduling.
- Increase the doom surface area by doing many experiments in parallel.

So it's not the classic Terminator scenario, but rather a case of AI making it
easier to find "[recipes for ruin][recipe]".

# Butlerian Jihad {#butler}

> JIHAD, BUTLERIAN: (_see also_ Great Revolt)---the crusade against computers,
> thinking machines, and conscious robots begun in 201 B.G. and concluded in 108
> B.G. Its chief commandment remains in the O.C. Bible as "Thou shalt not make a
> machine in the likeness of a human mind."
>
> --- Frank Herbert, [_Dune_][dune]

At the extreme end this is the actual Butlerian Jihad from _Dune_ (named,
tangentially, after [Samuel Butler][butler], who in _1863_ wrote [the first AI
doomer article][darwin]).

Otherwise, this is something like an international agreement to stop the
development of superintelligent AI ([ControlAI][ctrl] is advocating for this and
you should support them). Maybe this fails, and we go back to the start, or
succeeds and we can get on [the other road][road].

# Enduring Complementarity {#enduring-complementarity}

This is the mundane future. It turns out that the "G" in AGI was never
real. Every mind is its own _sui generis_ thing. Jaggedness remains. AIs remain
a complement to, rather than a substitute for, human labour. There is job
displacement but no mass unemployment, people move up to more
managerial/relational roles. There are fewer ICs and everyone is some kind of
manager. Labour becomes a smaller factor of production and consequently loses
some bargaining power against capital, but most people are fine.

# Permanent Underclass {#underclass}

In this future, we don't reach ASI, but non-superintelligent AI is enough to
automate almost all jobs. The labour term in the production function goes to
zero. All income is either capital ownership or welfare.

Economically: there may be material abundance, but absent some kind of Georgist
economic reform, most of the gains are captured by rent-seeking. Socially: there
is no more social mobility, your lot in life is determined by how much capital
you had when the music stopped. Growing your human capital is no longer
incentivized, since it doesn't change your life outcomes.

See also:

- [_A rising tide raises all rents_][rents] by [jyn].
- [_No-One Escapes the Permanent Underclass_][uc].

# The Singleton {#singleton}

> I am the Eschaton. I am not your god.\
> I am descended from you, and I exist in your future.\
> Thou shalt not violate causality within my historic light cone. Or else.
>
> --- Charles Stross, [_Singularity Sky_][ssky]

In this world, we build ASI, and it's successfully aligned to some impersonal
system of values. Then this first AI performs a [pivotal act][pivot], becoming
the [sysop of the universe][sysop]. Whatever value system the AI was built with
endures until heat death.

# Classic Doom {#classic-doom}

You know, the paperclip maximizer. "[The AI does not hate you, nor does it love
you, but you are made out of atoms which it can use for something
else][atoms]". This has been done to death so I won't bore you.

# Orion's Arm {#orions-arm}

> Another of the sayings of Chrysippus is: We should infer in the case of a
> beautiful dwelling-place that it was built for its owners and not for mice; we
> ought, therefore, in the same way to regard the universe as the dwelling-place
> of the gods.
>
> --- Cicero, [_De Natura Deorum_][cicero]

We build ASI, and it has its own goals and values. Luckily, it's neither
actively hostile to humans, nor so indifferent that it destroys us without even
noticing. It mostly leaves us alone, and goes off to another star to do its own
thing. The result might be something like the galactic ecology in [Orion's
Arm][oa], where humans and superintelligent AIs and everything in between have a
kind of stable co-existence by virtue of occupying different volumes of space.

# The World State {#world}

> ... [The] human race is best ordered when in all its movements and motors it
> is controlled by one Prince as by one mover, by one law as by one motion.
>
> --- Dante, [_De Monarchia_][dm]

In this world, a reliably intent-aligned ASI takes over the world. What happens
then depends on the character of its operator, who now wields the [ring of
Gyges][gyges].

# MAD World {#mad}

> As Nietzsche said, "God is dead". The gods die but the Titans gain
> power. Technology is just the clothes, the armour, of the Titans.
>
> --- Ernst Jünger

In this world, the [intent alignment][ia] problem is solved, and we can build an
ASI that reliably obeys the intent of its operator. Furthermore,
superintelligence is widely distributed rather than centralized: everyone has
their own personal intent-aligned ASI. In this world, everyone is 10,000x more
capable and maybe you can build WMDs with a prompt. What keeps the peace
is mutually-assured destruction. It's not clear how survivable this is.

The obvious failure mode is that one bad actor causes human extinction. Maybe
their ASI can't overpower all the other ASIs put together, _but_ it can do
enough damage to destroy human life before the other ASIs can act (a general
observation: the technology to make the Earth uninhabitable to humans is much
lower on the tech tree than the technology to build a distributed,
extinction-proof interstellar civilization).

The generalized ($n>1$ rather than $n=1$) version of this is that all conflicts
are multiplied: two humans having a feud is the equivalent of nuclear war, two
countries going to war is ????.

On the more positive note: if there's no way to stop AI, then "everyone should
have it" is obviously preferable to "only the Elect should have it", which is
intolerable. If the goal is to keep the peace, the threat of mutually-assured
destruction is better than totalitarianism, in the sense that there's more
dignity in it.

# Power Concentration {#power}

A smaller version of [MAD World](#mad), where intent-aligned ASI is less
distributed. A small number of actors have ASI, and everyone else just watches
what happens. Maybe the AI companies will divide the lightcone among themselves,
like in the [Treaty of Tordesillas][tord]: every star and galaxy between 0h and
12h of [right ascension][ra] goes to Anthropic, dually for OpenAI.
