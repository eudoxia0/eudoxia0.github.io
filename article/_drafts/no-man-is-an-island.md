---
title: No Man Is an Island
---

In this post, I argue:

1. Individual intellectual activity can only be sustained by participating in an intellectual community of other humans.
1. AI dissolves these communities, which in turn makes private intellectual activity harder and much rarer.
1. The post-AI future is one of greatly diminished intellectual activity.
1. Contemporary beliefs about the nature of motivation cause us to underrate the importance of community, and to underestimate the harm that AI will cause to intellectual activity.
